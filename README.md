PRIVACY POLICY — PATTERNS COLORING BOOK

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Patterns Coloring Book is a coloring app published by Wisimme. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Google Play edition of the app, including through its advertising service.

1. INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

We use this information to respond to your request and resolve technical problems. Please avoid sending sensitive information or identifying information about a child unless necessary to address your concern.

2. LOCAL APP DATA

Coloring activity takes place on your device. The app may retain local information needed for its features, such as preferences or coloring progress.

You can remove data stored in the app’s private storage by clearing its storage through Android settings or uninstalling it.

Images saved outside the app’s private storage, if supported and used, may remain on your device after uninstalling. These images can be managed through your device’s gallery or file manager.

3. ADVERTISING

Patterns Coloring Book uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share:

• IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
• App and advertisement interactions.
• Diagnostic and performance information.
• Device or app-related identifiers, subject to the SDK configuration and platform restrictions.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, maintain service performance, and prevent fraud or abuse.

The information processed depends on the SDK version, advertising settings, and applicable restrictions. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no technical information is processed.

Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about Google advertising technologies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Patterns Coloring Book is designed for a family audience, including children under six years old.

The app is not designed to invite children to submit identifying personal information. Parents or guardians should handle support communications on behalf of young children.

The child-directed edition uses advertising requests configured for child-directed treatment, users under the age of consent, non-personalized advertisements, and Google’s G advertising content rating filter.

These protections restrict advertising behavior but do not eliminate all technical data processing by the advertising service.

Parents or guardians may contact Wisimme with questions about children’s privacy or to request deletion of information they have provided directly.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Google may receive information directly through its advertising SDK and process it on servers outside your country, subject to its policies and applicable safeguards.

Advertisements may lead to external websites or apps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not govern their practices.

6. RETENTION AND DELETION

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed or overwritten.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you have provided directly by contacting us.

Google determines retention of information processed through its services under its applicable policies. Clearing local app data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.

7. SECURITY

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information.

Contact us for requests concerning information held by Wisimme. For information processed by Google, consult Google’s Privacy Policy and available privacy controls.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will display a revised date.
